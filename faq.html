Getting started

What is DrixPay?

DrixPay is a financial app that combines a multi-currency wallet, crypto cards, cross-border transfers and currency exchange. It is designed for everyday users rather than professional investors.

Which devices are supported?

DrixPay is available on iOS and Android and as a web app. Please download the official app from our download page.

How do I register?

Open the app, register with your mobile number and follow the instructions to complete KYC identity verification. All features are available after verification.

Why is KYC verification required?

All users must complete identity verification and risk assessment procedures in accordance with applicable laws and regulations. This is a KYC and AML requirement and also helps protect your account.

Android shows an "unknown sources" warning. What should I do?

Temporarily enable "Allow installation from unknown sources" in Settings > Security for this official download only, and disable it again after installation. If the download is interrupted or installation fails, please contact support@drixpay.com.

Cards

How do I apply for a card?

After completing KYC verification, you can apply for a card in the app.

Where can I use the card?

The DrixPay Card runs on the Visa and Mastercard networks and can be used at merchants that accept them, online and in store.

What is the difference between virtual and physical cards?

A virtual card can be used for online payments once issued. A physical card can also be used for in-store payments. Available card types and how to request them are shown in the app.

What are the card fees and limits?

Fees, limits and availability vary by card type and region. Please refer to the app for current details.

Top-up and transfers

How do I top up?

You can top up through crypto or fiat channels. Your balance can then be used for card payments, transfers and currency exchange.

Which currencies are supported?

DrixPay supports major stablecoins and crypto assets. The current list of currencies and networks is shown on the top-up page in the app.

Do I pay gas fees on transfers?

No. The Still Protocol allows on-chain transfers without gas fees, so you do not need to hold additional tokens to pay network fees.

How long do transfers take?

Transfers are settled on the FUSN public chain and normally arrive within seconds. Each transfer can be traced on-chain.

How are exchange rates set?

DrixPay exchange rates are synchronized with global banks in real time. The full quote is shown before you confirm, and each exchange is routed through the best available path.

Security and compliance

Does DrixPay promise 600% annualized returns?

No. DrixPay has never promised any fixed returns or fixed annualized returns.

Does DrixPay guarantee principal and returns?

No. DrixPay has never provided any user with principal or return guarantees.

Does DrixPay guarantee profits?

No. All market activities involve risk.

Does DrixPay implement KYC and AML requirements?

Yes. DrixPay strictly implements KYC, KYB, AML and related compliance requirements.

Is DrixPay supervised as part of a listed company?

Yes. DrixPay is part of a Hong Kong listed company group and is subject to its governance and compliance oversight.

Are online claims about high returns, static income or guaranteed doubling true?

No. Such content does not represent DrixPay's official position.

Will DrixPay take legal action against defamation and infringement?

Yes. The company will continue to protect its own and its users' legitimate rights through legal letters, complaints, litigation and other lawful means.

How can I identify official DrixPay channels?

Please rely on information published through DrixPay's official website, announcements, app and certified channels. The full list of official domains is available under Official channels.

How are user assets protected?

DrixPay uses cold and hot wallet separation, dual encryption and an AI risk engine, and applies KYC, KYB, AML and CFT procedures. See Security and Compliance.
