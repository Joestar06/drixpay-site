Solemn Statement on Dissemination of False Information Online and DrixPay's Compliance Operations

Published: June 23, 2026 DrixPay Legal & Compliance Department
I. No fixed returns II. Legal and compliant operations III. Listed company oversight IV. Infringement and defamation V. Official information sources

Recently, our company has discovered that certain websites, self-media accounts, forums, public accounts, and communities have been disseminating false information involving DrixPay, including so-called "600% annualized returns," "fixed returns," "principal protection & guaranteed returns," "static dividends," and "price only goes up." This exaggerated or fabricated content seriously misleads public perception and damages the company's brand reputation and user rights.

In response, DrixPay solemnly declares as follows:

I. DrixPay Has Never Promised Any Fixed Returns

DrixPay has never, in any form, promised any individual or institution:

  • Fixed returns;
  • Principal protection or guaranteed returns;
  • Annualized rate of return;
  • Static income;
  • Guaranteed profit;
  • Guaranteed principal recovery;
  • Guaranteed multiple returns;
  • Guaranteed price increase.
Any return propaganda, return calculations, price predictions, and return commitments circulating online do not represent the official position of DrixPay.

II. DrixPay Adheres to Legal and Compliant Operations

DrixPay is a technology service enterprise focused on global payments, cross-border settlement, and digital financial infrastructure development.

As a payment and settlement service provider, DrixPay consistently upholds: service to customers, compliant operations, honesty and integrity, and law-abiding conduct.

The company strictly implements:

  • KYC (Know Your Customer)
  • KYB (Know Your Business)
  • AML (Anti-Money Laundering)
  • CFT (Counter-Financing of Terrorism)

and other compliance systems, while continuously advancing the global compliance framework.

All users must complete identity verification and risk assessment procedures in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.

III. Supervised Operations Under Listed Company System

DrixPay is integrated into the management system of a Hong Kong listed company and is subject to the supervision of listed company governance structures, internal audit systems, and compliance management frameworks.

All business development, marketing, brand promotion, and partnership activities of the company must comply with internal compliance management requirements.

Any unauthorized return propaganda, exaggerated marketing, false promises, and misleading statements do not represent the company's position.

IV. Regarding Infringement and Defamation

The company has continuously taken measures to protect its rights against malicious defamation, slanderous attacks, impersonation of official identity, false promotion, and dissemination of false information.

To date, some infringing content has been removed, some responsible parties have received legal letters, and some cases have entered legal proceedings.

For ongoing infringement, malicious dissemination of false information, damage to company reputation, and misleading of the public, the company will take legal actions including but not limited to:

  • Legal letter warnings;
  • Platform complaints and reports;
  • Civil litigation;
  • Goodwill and reputation infringement liability;
  • Criminal complaints;
  • Cross-border judicial cooperation.

The company reserves the right to pursue all legal liabilities against relevant parties in accordance with the law.

V. Official Information Sources

Users and partners are advised to rely on information published through DrixPay's official website, official announcements, official app, and officially certified channels. The list of official domains is available under Official channels.

Any third-party articles, videos, community chat records, marketing materials, and personal interpretations do not represent the official position of DrixPay.

Hereby declared.
